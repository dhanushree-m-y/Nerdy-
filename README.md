# NUMI – The World Runs on Math

A K–5 math adventure for kids aged 6–11, built with React Native and Expo SDK 57. Children fix the world of Numbershire by building, sharing, cutting and grouping things. NUMI, a voice learning companion, watches how they work, helps when they get stuck, and asks them to explain their thinking.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npx expo start          # press a = Android, i = iOS, w = web
```

- **Expo Go:** everything works except speech recognition (see *Voice* below).
- **Web:** `npx expo start --web` shows the app in a phone-width frame.
- **Reset progress:** open Grown-ups (PIN) → Reset progress.

## The core scenario (spec §47)

World map → Number Village → "OH NO!" → Bridge Builder:

1. The child places a wrong plank. The game gives soft feedback: a wobble and a description of what the plank makes.
2. The child tries again, then pauses. NUMI notices and offers help ("Hmm… this one's tricky").
3. The child picks **Talk it through** and says "I don't understand". NUMI answers from the real bridge: "You already built 8. We need 15… how many more would make 10?"
4. The child answers **2**, then **5**, while the matching blocks glow. They drag in the **7** and the bridge repairs itself. Nia crosses, and the equation appears: `8 + 2 + 5 = 15` → `8 + 7 = 15`.
5. NUMI asks "How did you figure that out?" and recognises the strategy: **NEW STRATEGY DISCOVERED — Make a Ten**.
6. Confidence check-in → Reward → the world changes (bridge rebuilt, Market Street unlocked) → a mastery moment → back to the map, where the change is visible.

## Sound & voice

**Tone:** calm. NUMI reads at a low-ish pitch and an unhurried pace, sentence by sentence with a small breath between each, and the sound effects are soft mallet and bell tones rather than arcade beeps.

- **Sound effects** live in `assets/sfx` and are generated by `node scripts/make-sfx.js` — short WAVs synthesised from sine partials with gentle envelopes, so they're ours and tiny. Playback is `src/services/sfx.ts`; every effect is mixed low and ducks while NUMI talks.
- **Where they play:** a wooden tick on taps, a snap when a piece lands, a two-note rise for correct, a soft low tone for "not quite", an arpeggio for mission complete, a bell for a new strategy, a rising figure for a power level-up, and a quiet meadow pad on the world map.
- **Read-aloud:** tap any `SpeakableText` or the 🔊 `SpeakButton` to hear it. On Explorer 1, goals read themselves once.
- **Everything is optional.** In the Explorer tab: Sounds, Background music, NUMI's voice, "Read things to me automatically", voice style (Calm/Playful) and speed. The same controls, plus microphone and camera, are in the grown-up area. With sound off, captions still animate at reading pace.

- **NUMI speaks** using `expo-speech` on every platform. The caption always shows too.
- **Speech recognition** uses the Web Speech API where it exists (Chrome and Edge on web). If the mic isn't available (Expo Go, no permission, or turned off in grown-up settings), NUMI switches to "say-it" chips: the child taps what they would say. Every voice action also works by touch.
- **Adding recognition on phones:** make a development build and connect a recognizer module (e.g. `expo-speech-recognition`) inside `src/services/listen.ts`. The rest of the app already works through that file.

## Replay & pacing

- **Endless rounds:** `src/learning/generator.ts` builds fresh, seeded puzzles per mission. Difficulty is chosen from the child's own recent signals (clean and quick → harder; repeated struggle → easier), and the starting representation follows `nextRepresentation()`, so a struggling child begins with blocks or groups already showing.
- **Play another** appears beside Continue after a win, so a mission can run as long as the child is enjoying it.
- **First-play tips** (`FirstTimeTip`) appear once per place and are remembered in `seenTips`.
- **Break reminder:** a gentle "time to stretch" card after the interval grown-ups choose (`settings.breakMinutes`, 0 turns it off). It never locks the child out.
- **Seasons:** when every area is restored, the map celebrates and offers a new season — powers and backpack stay, the places need help again with new puzzles.

## Accessibility and safety

- Grown-ups can turn on reduced motion (Reanimated's `ReducedMotionConfig` plus the app's own motion checks), large touch targets, captions, a speech-speed setting, and sound and haptics switches.
- Feedback never relies on colour alone.
- The microphone and camera are off unless a grown-up allows them.
- NUMI steers off-topic chat back to the puzzle.
- There are no leaderboards.
- The camera flow in Math Around Me uses an illustrated sample photo; no camera module is installed yet.
